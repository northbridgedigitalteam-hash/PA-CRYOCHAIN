    Help & FAQs

    How are PPECB statuses updated in this system?

    PA-CryoChain reflects PPECB statuses based on official inspection results, NCRs and cooling confirmations received from PPECB and your own operational records. This system does not replace official PPECB communications.

    How are DALRRD/eCert statuses updated?

    DALRRD and eCert statuses are updated once phytosanitary certificates, approvals and related documentation are received. For legal or regulatory questions, please confirm directly with DALRRD.

    Which commodities are supported?

    PA-CryoChain currently supports citrus, deciduous, exotic and stone fruit types, with commodity-based temperature targets and workflow logic.

    Who do I contact for support?

    Please contact your operations team or PA support for assistance. You may also log a query through your usual communication channels.
